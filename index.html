        Troubleshooting Windows, NICE CXone & Avaya Audio
        • Share Entire Screen: In Chrome's prompt, select the Entire Screen tab (not an individual Window) and switch ON Also share system audio.
        • Playback vs. Communication Endpoint: Avaya and NICE CXone softphones often send call audio to Windows' Default Communications Device. Click the speaker icon on your Windows taskbar (no admin needed) and confirm your active output device is the same USB/Bluetooth/wired headset playing the call.
        • Harmless Audio First: Always verify audio levels and speech clarity with harmless test audio before using during authorized live calls.
        • No Microphone Fallback: By strict privacy design, this tool never switches to your microphone if system audio is missing.
