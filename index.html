        Troubleshooting Windows, NICE CXone & Avaya Audio
        • Avaya Background Audio Mute: Keep Avaya's Mute background sound setting turned OFF (and Windows Sound → Communications set to Do nothing) so Windows loopback receives call audio.
        • Share Entire Screen: In Chrome's prompt, select Entire Screen and switch ON Also share system audio.
        • Default Playback vs. Communications Device: Press Win + R → mmsys.cpl and ensure your headset has both the Green Checkmark and Green Telephone icon.
        • No Microphone Fallback: By strict privacy design, this tool never switches to your microphone.
